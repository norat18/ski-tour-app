📖 How to Use PNW Ski Tours

1

Select Your Zone

Choose the NWAC avalanche zone where you want to tour. The app covers all 10 zones from Mt. Baker to Mt. Hood. This determines which tours and forecasts you'll see.

2

Review Conditions

Check the Avalanche Forecast (from NWAC), Weather (from NWS), and Snow Report (from Synoptic weather stations, or USDA SNOTEL when Synoptic is unavailable). These update automatically for your selected zone and date.

3

Set Your Profile

Use the Energy Level and Skill Level sliders to tell the app about yourself today. This helps match you with appropriate tours - higher fitness = longer tours, higher skill = more technical terrain.

4

Browse Tour Recommendations

Tours are sorted by how well they match you and today's conditions. Each card shows:

  • Match score - How well the tour fits today
  • Distance & elevation - Tour statistics
  • Difficulty ratings - Fitness and skill required
  • Aspect & elevation - Terrain characteristics
  • Bonuses/warnings - Highlights and cautions
5

Plan Your Tour

Click 🗺️ View Map to search CalTopo for route information, or Directions 🗺️ for Google Maps driving directions to the trailhead. Use the date picker to plan up to 7 days ahead.

6

Log Your Tours

After your tour, click 📝 Log Tour to record conditions using the AIARE debrief framework. Track avalanche danger, snow conditions, signs of instability, and lessons learned. Sign in to sync your tour logs across devices.

⚠️ Important Safety Note

This app is a planning aid, not a substitute for avalanche education, proper equipment, and sound decision-making. Always check the official NWAC forecast, carry rescue gear, and travel with experienced partners.
